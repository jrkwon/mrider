# 1. Odometry

---

# Integrating your way to a position

$$ \dot{x} = v\cos\theta \qquad \dot{y} = v\sin\theta \qquad \dot{\theta} = \frac{v\tan\delta}{L} $$

Two constants turn raw counts into those quantities:

- `meters_per_tick`
- steering counts → radians, from the absolute sensor

---

# MRider refuses to derive `meters_per_tick`

**Roll-out calibration:**

- Drive a **measured** straight distance *L*
- Record the tick delta
- `meters_per_tick = L / Δticks`
- Three times, averaged

**Authoritative because it bypasses guessing.**

No gear ratio. No quadrature decode factor. No tyre diameter under load.
All of them fold into one measured number.

---

# Which is why F7 can forbid a PPR figure

The source project:

- firmware pins **52 PPR**
- its own BOM lists a **16 PPR** motor

A factor-of-three disagreement nobody caught.

**Measure yours.** The roll-out makes the resulting distance safe either way —
that is exactly why the calibration is designed that way.

---

# Why it drifts

| Error | Effect |
|---|---|
| Wheel slip | Rotation without ground motion — always over-estimates |
| Tyre deformation | 6 kg of payload on a 10 kg vehicle |
| Gearbox backlash | The encoder is upstream and cannot see it |
| Steering angle error | **Heading error — grows unbounded** |

**1° of heading bias = 0.35 m off after 20 m.**

And it keeps growing whether or not you keep making the error.

---

# MRider's measurement is narrower still

- Both rear motors **in parallel** on one driver channel
- Only **one** carries an encoder — ADR C
- Measurement taken at **one motor shaft**

In a turn, the two rear wheels genuinely travel different distances.

**One of them is simply not observed.** Its speed is inferred.

# 2. The EKF

---

# Fuse velocities, not pose

```yaml
odom0_config: [false, false, false,   # x, y, z   <- NOT fused
               false, false, false,   # roll, pitch, yaw
               true,  true,  false,   # vx, vy, vz  <- fused
               false, false, true,    # vyaw
               false, false, false]
```

> *Integrated wheel position drifts without bound and there is
> no absolute reference indoors to correct it.*

Feed it an already-drifted pose and it fuses the drift **as evidence.**

---

# Take the IMU's yaw, not its accelerations

> *On a vibrating ride-on chassis they are mostly drivetrain buzz.*

A sensor axis you **can** read is not automatically one you **should** fuse.

Also `imu0_relative: true` — no magnetometer heading reference is
trusted indoors, near steel and motors.

---

# The Lisbon declination

B-MROVER's `navsat_transform` carried a magnetic declination set for **Lisbon**.

Wrong here — and it would **silently rotate the global frame.**

MRider's indoor profile does not fix the number.
**It drops the GNSS EKF entirely.**

---

<!-- _class: quote -->

> The choice was not between a right number and a wrong number. It was between an unverifiable number and deleting the thing that needed it.

Remember this move. It comes back in Week 8.

---

# Transform ownership

| Transform | Owner |
|---|---|
| `map → odom` | `slam_toolbox` |
| `odom → base_link` | EKF |
| `base_link → *` | `robot_state_publisher` |

`enable_odom_tf: false` on the controller, so the EKF is the **sole** publisher.

**The gates this serves:**
≤ 2% drift over 20 m straight · ≤ 5° heading error on a closed 20 m figure-eight

**Nobody has measured either.** That is `mitt_bench` — Simulation & Validation.
