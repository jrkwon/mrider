# The safety gate comes before the rubric

Bench before vehicle. Wheels-off before wheels-on.
Walking pace before anything faster.

A team that skipped a stage does not lose 10 points.
**It does not drive.** It presents, and demonstrates on stands.

---

<!-- _class: dense -->

# Before the first run

- Clear area marked; everyone without a role behind the line
- E-stop pressed once: traction cuts, vehicle reverts to STOCK
- RC override taken once under a laptop command
- `MITT_VEHICLE=<n>`, `vehicle:=<n>`, `use_sim_time:=false`
- No `WRONG VEHICLE` block
- `/mitt/dbw/status` at ≥ 50 Hz
- Map loaded, localization converged
- For a goal that needs reverse: `/scan` shows the space behind

---

# Each run

**Operator** — RC transmitter, thumbs on the sticks
**Spotter** — hand on the E-stop, eyes on the vehicle and the people
**Engineer** — at the laptop, the only one who sends commands

*"Commanding." / "Ready." / "Clear." / "Go."*

Afterwards, before anything else: **write the outcome down.**

---

# When it goes wrong

Stop. Say what you saw. Say what you think it is.

Halting a run with the E-stop at the right moment
**is** the safety rubric.

Letting it continue to see whether it recovers is not.
